# Provenance

This app's design and code are its own. No code was copied from another project.

- Its shape follows common retail practice: click and collect against a pickup code, and franchise
  royalties as a percentage of reported sales.
- The orders, delivery notes, stock and invoices are ERP's, used through the platform's records.
- Store replenishment is ERP Procurement's auto-reorder, not reimplemented here.
